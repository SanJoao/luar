A Place Where Ideas Are Born

This is the long game: Luarai as a live-in incubator. A real, physical home where builders live for free, with food, tools, and services covered, and pour everything into their ideas. In exchange? A small slice of what they build (think ~10%). Everybody wins. Especially the ideas.

Where We Are Today

Full honesty: we can't pay for anyone's groceries yet. Today, Luarai is a tiny product studio building its own things. Everything we've shipped and experimented with lives in our portfolio. The incubator isn't open. There's no application form. This page exists because we'd rather say the dream out loud than keep it in a drawer.
